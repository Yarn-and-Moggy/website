Privacy & Your Data

Last Updated: March 2026

Your Basket

Your shopping basket is stored locally in your browser. We won't see it unless you place an order.

Payment & Personal Details

Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We don't see or store your card details, name, or address - Stripe does. They're the ones responsible for keeping it safe, and they're very good at it.

Data We Do Hold

We only hold a reference to your Stripe checkout ID. This allows us to retrieve your name, address and phone number so we can process your order and supply tracking information.

We don't store your personal data. We only process it for the purposes of fulfilling orders and sending an email thanking you for your order.

Marketing

We don't use mailing lists. The only time you'll hear from us is regarding your order.
