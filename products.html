Shop / Product

All Yarn & Moggy toys are made with 100% cotton yarn and potent organic catnipt for maximum engagement.

We are working towards using all our waste yarn to create recycled stuffing, though some regular polyester toy stuffing may still be used during this transitional period

Every item in our shop is crocheted by hand — a machine that can crochet does not exist. A single toy can take several hours to make. The price reflects the time, skill, and love that goes into each one.

All our toys are made with cat safety in mind. We use pet-safe materials throughout. As with any toy, we recommend supervised play and checking regularly for wear and tear.
