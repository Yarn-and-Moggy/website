Good to Know

Everything you might want to ask

How long will my order take?

Some items are made to order by hand, so please allow up to 7 days for dispatch. Once dispatched, we ship via Royal Mail Tracked 48 — typically 2-3 business days. You'll receive an email when your order is on its way.

Why does crochet cost more?

Every item in our shop is crocheted by hand — a machine that can crochet does not exist. A single toy can take several hours to make. The price reflects the time, skill, and love that goes into each one.

Are the toys safe for my cat?

All our toys are made with cat safety in mind. We use pet-safe materials throughout. As with any toy, we recommend supervised play and checking regularly for wear and tear.

Can I request a custom order?

Sometimes! Drop us a message on Instagram and we'll let you know what's possible.
